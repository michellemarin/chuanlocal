Welcome, in your language.

ชวน(chuan) means “to invite.” Every sign is an invitation.

The problem

A lost sale looks like a quiet day.

Visitors can’t read Thai-only signs, so they point, guess or walk past. The vendor never sees the sale they lost.

The idea

The vendor’s Thai stays. Yours sits beside it.

The vendor never leaves Thai. Visitors read the list in their own language, with the Thai underneath to point at.

How it works · Live demo

  1. 01Type the list in ThaiOn the vendor’s phone. No app, no account.
  2. 02Put the sign on the wallOne QR sign. One tap changes today’s special.
  3. 03Scan to read it in your languageOpens in the visitor’s phone language, Thai underneath.
